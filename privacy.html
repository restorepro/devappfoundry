OpenCode Desk — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What OpenCode Desk is

OpenCode Desk is a macOS desktop application that lets a user publish and manage their own content across the social media accounts they personally connect to it (for example: Bluesky, YouTube, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, Pinterest, and Gumroad).

What data the app accesses

When you connect a social platform, OpenCode Desk uses that platform's official OAuth flow. You sign in directly with the platform, and the app receives an access token limited to the permissions you approve (for example, permission to post to your own account).

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your Mac, in the macOS Keychain, under the app's private keychain service. Tokens are never transmitted to any server operated by us, and are never shared, sold, or used for analytics.
  • Content you choose to publish (text, images, video) is sent directly from your Mac to the platform you selected. It is not stored on our servers.
  • No third-party tracking. The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics collectors, and no tracking of any kind.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to ourselves or any third party.
  • We do not access your accounts except to perform the actions you explicitly trigger in the app.
  • We do not sell or share tokens, content, or account information.

Account connection & revocation

You can disconnect any platform at any time, either inside the app or by revoking the app's access from that platform's account settings (for example, "Apps and Website Permissions" on Instagram/Threads, "Connected apps" on TikTok, "Connected accounts" on Pinterest). Revoking access invalidates the token stored on your Mac.

Data deletion

Because all credentials are stored locally, uninstalling the app and emptying the Trash removes all stored tokens from your machine. If you would like confirmation that any data associated with your use of the app has been deleted from our side, contact us at the address below — we retain no user data server-side.
