SecureVault Private Storage Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

SecureVault Private Storage stores personal photos, videos, documents, downloads, and screen recordings locally on your iPhone or iPad.

Data collection

The app does not require an account. Dev App Foundry does not operate advertising, analytics, or tracking services in the app and does not collect or sell personal information through the app.

Local storage and security

Files you import, download, or record are stored locally in the app’s private encrypted vault. Vault credential and security metadata are protected using Apple’s iOS Keychain. Content remains on your device unless you intentionally export or share it.

Device permissions

Photo-library access is used to import or export selected media. Microphone access is used only when you enable audio while making a screen recording. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Secure browser and third parties

The in-app browser connects to websites you choose to visit. Those websites and downloads are governed by their own privacy practices.

Passwords, deletion, and backups

You are responsible for remembering your vault password and maintaining backups of important originals. Dev App Foundry cannot recover your password or decrypt inaccessible vault content. Deleting the app may permanently remove locally stored vault data.

Children

SecureVault is a general-purpose storage utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

Revisions will appear here with an updated effective date. For questions, visit SecureVault Support.
